      Sources — read 21 Nov 2026

        How this guide was checked

        Names, descriptions and grades are the official list (Exophase / PSNProfiles agree on all 55). Every guide is written in our own words; sources are cited, never copied. Where sources disagree, both versions are shown — with our call. Counts come from PSN's own trackers. Five hidden trophies are still unconfirmed — they are labelled, not guessed.

        Platinum estimate: ~200 hours, difficulty 4/10 (PowerPyx [1]); Push Square reports 300+ hours [7] — see dispute D1. One playthrough, no online trophies, no mission replay — keep a manual save at the start and end of every chapter.

        Roadmap to the platinum — 6 stages, ~200 hours

        Built from the cited guides: story chapters first (Chapters 1–2, then 3–5), then the 100% completion checklist in free-roam, then strangers and misc cleanup, then money, property and the platinum — and a sixth stage, the victory lap: verify the run, export it and share it. One playthrough. No online trophies. No mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start and end of every chapter. Cheats disable trophies and saving — keep them off this save [1].

        Missables & risk — what can actually go wrong

        Only one trophy is flagged possibly missable by the guides. Thirteen more are at risk because their method opens through a chapter-gated optional mission or a character-only chain — do those when they appear. Five hidden trophies are still unconfirmed. Everything else is safe to clean up after the story in free-roam [1][2].

        Disputes — where the sources disagree, both versions and our call

        Eighty-eight disagreements found while reading the 32 sources. Each shows version A, version B, and the verdict this guide follows. A dispute is not a guess: the verdict names the side with the stronger evidence.

        FAQ — the questions players ask first, every answer cited

        Forty-nine questions, each answered from the numbered sources — the same citation rule as every guide step. Where the sources disagree, the answer says so and points at the dispute. The glossary below it defines the thirty-two terms the trophy list keeps using.

        Myths — fifteen things players believe that the sources contradict

        A myth is not a dispute: a dispute is two sources disagreeing with each other, a myth is the community disagreeing with the sources. Each card states the myth, then the reality with its citations. Where a myth is also a numbered dispute, the reality points at it.

        Grind routes — the twelve long counters, one route each

        Every grind in the game has a named route: where to go, what the method is, and the one tip that saves the most time. The targets here are the ledger's targets, so the two tabs always agree. Below the routes: eight launch-week patch notes that pin the guide's numbers to their dates, and ten save points that say exactly which slot to use and why.

        Save points — which slot, and why
